Option 1 — Focus on one ion

Tap an ion in the key; the stage redraws that ion alone on its own scale. The dilute side gets a countable handful; the concentrated side, where dots would not fit, becomes a haze with its ratio printed.

Option 2 — A census of one cubic micrometer

Real counts in a real volume, from the model's concentrations (1 mM = 602,214 ions per µm³). Inside, every free Ca²⁺ is drawn.

Option 3 — A magnifier on the membrane

A lens on one patch of membrane: a 100 nm cube on each side, one dot per 500 ions. At rest the inside usually holds no free Ca²⁺ at all.
