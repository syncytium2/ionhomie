Mockup for Tony: carbonic anhydrase II from the enzyme's point of view. The active site is a schematic after Domsic et al. 2008, Fig. 6 (not to scale), with the proton path to His64 from their text. Sourced: the zinc's three histidines, the hydrophobic pocket and its residues, the carbon 2.8 Å from the zinc's OH⁻, the displaced deep water, the zinc water's pKa near 7, CO₂ bound weakly (Kd ≈ 100 mM), a turnover up to about 10⁶ s⁻¹. The individual step rates are illustrative, chosen so that the cycle obeys the equation's equilibrium (pK 6.1).
