No pump moves Cl⁻. In the cell so far it has simply settled wherever Vm put it: E_Cl equal to Vm, a passive distribution. Two cotransporters change that, each running on a gradient the Na⁺/K⁺ pump built. NKCC1 carries 1 Na⁺, 1 K⁺ and 2 Cl⁻ in together, riding the Na⁺ gradient. KCC2 carries 1 K⁺ and 1 Cl⁻ out together, riding the K⁺ gradient. Neither moves any net charge.

E_Cl matters because GABAA receptors are Cl⁻ channels. Where E_Cl sits relative to Vm decides whether GABA hyperpolarizes a neuron or depolarizes it.

Predict first. Switch on KCC2 alone. How far down can it push E_Cl: as far as you like, or is there a floor? Then try NKCC1 alone. Does Vm move much while E_Cl moves?

What to notice

  • KCC2 has a floor. It pushes Cl⁻ out only while the K⁺ gradient pays for it, so E_Cl can approach E_K but never pass it. That is why adult cortical E_GABA sits between E_K and Vm, around −80 mV.
  • NKCC1 loads Cl⁻ above passive, paid for by the Na⁺ gradient, and E_Cl rises above Vm. Opening GABAA channels then lets Cl⁻ out, and the cell depolarizes. In this cell, NKCC1 strong enough to bring E_Cl to −36.5 mV, the E_GABA measured in adult mouse GnRH neurons, puts it well above a typical spike threshold.
  • Vm hardly moves while E_Cl moves tens of mV. The cotransporters carry no net charge, and Cl⁻ is a small part of the resting permeability. E_Cl is a setting the cell can change without disturbing its resting potential.
  • NKCC1 swells the cell: it brings in four ions at a time, and water follows. Cells use exactly this to recover their volume after shrinking.
  • Everything here stands on the pump. Block it on the pumps page and both transporters lose the gradients they run on.
The model, and what it does not claim

Each cotransporter's rate is proportional to the logarithm of its driving ratio, so it runs forward, stops, or reverses exactly as thermodynamics allows:

NKCC1 inward ∝ ln( [Na]o[K]o[Cl]o² / [Na]i[K]i[Cl]i² ) KCC2 outward ∝ ln( [K]i[Cl]i / [K]o[Cl]o )

E_GABA is taken to equal E_Cl. Real GABAA channels also pass some bicarbonate (HCO₃⁻), which pulls E_GABA a little positive of E_Cl; this model leaves bicarbonate out.

The cell is generic. The two presets show what transport could bring this cell to each E_GABA; they do not claim what any real neuron expresses. The −36.5 mV figure is the E_GABA measured with gramicidin perforated patch in adult mouse GnRH neurons (DeFazio et al. 2002, Mol Endocrinol 16:2872), the recording mode that leaves internal Cl⁻ undisturbed. Internal Cl⁻ in those cells was not measured directly, and which transporter sets it there is not claimed here.
