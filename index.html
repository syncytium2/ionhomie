ionhomie

Ion homeostasis, built one mechanism at a time, in the browser. Diffusion and Fick's law; drift in a field and the Nernst equation; a membrane as a capacitor; a selective channel; the Goldman–Hodgkin–Katz equation; the rundown of a cell with no pump; the Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase and PMCA; and the NKCC1 and KCC2 cotransporters that set ECl and with it EGABA. For graduate and medical students. Source: github.com/syncytium2/ionhomie (MIT).
